The Salon

An institution,
not a franchise.

Tucked beneath the rotunda of Clock Tower Plaza, Salon Bené has been quietly perfecting hair for decades. No conveyor belt of clients, no stranger with your file — just Janet behind the chair, Bill at the desk, and the kind of attention that only an owner-run salon can give.

Janet is, in her clients’ words, an artist — a stylist who reads what your hair wants to do and makes it do it better. Bill keeps the coffee excellent, the music restful and the welcome warm. Come once and you’re a client; come twice and you’re family.
